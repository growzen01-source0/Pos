Point of Sale · Android · Rs.

Ring up orders before the fries go cold.

FlamePOS is a billing app built for busy fast-food counters. Tap items, take payment, print the receipt — all in Pakistani Rupees, all offline on your Android tablet or phone.

Get FlamePOS See what it does

Everything the counter needs.

01

PIN lock

Lock the register between shifts. Only staff with the PIN can open billing.

02

Ready-made menu

Starts with pizzas, shawarmas, zinger burgers and fries already priced — open and start selling.

03

Fast cart checkout

Tap to add, adjust quantities, and total the bill in Rs. in seconds.

04

Receipts

Clean receipts with your shop name and a custom thank-you footer.

05

Menu editor

Add items, change prices and reorganise categories whenever your menu changes.

06

Udhar ledger

Keep track of credit customers and what they owe, without a paper khata.

07

Daily sales history

See every order and the day's takings at a glance when you close up.

08

Works offline

Everything is stored on the device, so a dropped connection never stops a sale.
